Vinyl Flooring FAQs

Do you remove and haul away old flooring?
Yes—select “Old Flooring Removal & Haul-Away” and we’ll include it in your quote.
Can you repair or level the subfloor?
Yes—we handle minor repairs and leveling. Mention it in the details box.
What areas do you service?
Greater St. Louis and surrounding areas. Call to confirm your exact location.
Do you offer warranties?
Yes—labor warranty plus manufacturer warranty on materials.
What type of vinyl is best for high-traffic areas?
LVP/LVT with a thicker wear layer is most durable for busy homes and pets.
Can vinyl be installed over my existing floor?
Often yes—over tile, hardwood, or concrete if level and dry. We’ll prep as needed.
How long does installation take?
Most projects finish in 1–3 days depending on rooms and prep.
Is vinyl flooring waterproof?
Modern LVP/LVT is fully waterproof—great for basements, baths, and kitchens.
What maintenance is required?
Sweep/vacuum and damp-mop with a pH-neutral cleaner—no waxing needed.
Can I choose custom patterns or borders?
Yes—decorative inlays, borders, and mixed layouts are available.
