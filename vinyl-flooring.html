Vinyl Flooring FAQs

Do you remove and haul away old flooring?
Yes—select “Old Flooring Removal & Haul-Away” and we’ll include it in your quote.
Can you repair or level the subfloor?
Yes—we handle minor repairs and leveling. Mention it in the details box.
What areas do you service?
Greater St. Louis and surrounding areas. Call to confirm your exact location.
Do you offer warranties?
Yes—labor warranty plus manufacturer warranty on materials.
What type of vinyl is best for high-traffic areas?
Luxury Vinyl Plank (LVP) or Tile (LVT) with a thick wear layer is the most durable option for busy households, pets, and commercial spaces.
Can vinyl flooring be installed over my existing floor?
In many cases yes—vinyl can go over tile, hardwood, or concrete as long as the surface is level and dry. We’ll inspect and prep the subfloor as needed.
How long does installation usually take?
Most small to medium-size projects are completed in 1–3 days depending on room count, subfloor preparation, and material selection.
Is vinyl flooring waterproof?
Most modern LVP and LVT products are fully waterproof, making them ideal for basements, bathrooms, kitchens, and laundry rooms.
What kind of maintenance does vinyl flooring require?
Simply sweep or vacuum regularly and damp-mop with a pH-neutral cleaner—no waxing or polishing required.
Can I choose custom patterns or borders?
Yes, we can create decorative inlays, borders, or mixed-pattern layouts to give your space a unique look.
