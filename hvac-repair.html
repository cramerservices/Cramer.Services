Common HVAC Problems We Fix

❄️
AC blows warm air
Could be low refrigerant, a dirty coil, or a failed capacitor.
🔥
Furnace short cycling
Often a dirty filter, flame sensor, or pressure switch.
🌀
Weak airflow
Duct leaks, clogged filters, or blower motor issues.
💧
Water near unit
Clogged drain line, frozen coil, or condensate pump failure.
⚡
System won’t start
Thermostat, breaker, contactor, or control board problems.
🔊
Strange noises
Bearings, fan blades, or debris contacting the blower.
