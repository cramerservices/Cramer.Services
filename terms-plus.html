Description of Services

Under this subscription, Cramer Services will provide one (1) AC tune-up per year and one (1) Furnace tune-up per year. The tune-ups include:

  • Air filter inspection and replacement (standard filter included)
  • Refrigerant level check
  • Inspection of electrical components
  • System safety checks
  • Interior and exterior coil cleaning
  • Cleaning of blower components
  • Cleaning of drain components
  • Inspection and cleaning of burners

Benefits: Priority Service (scheduling priority) and a 10% credit toward future repairs and installations performed by Cramer Services. Credits are automatically applied to the customer’s invoice and are not redeemable for cash.

Exclusions: Replacement parts (other than the included standard air filter) and repairs are not included in this subscription. Any additional parts or repair services identified during inspection will be quoted separately and billed to the customer.
