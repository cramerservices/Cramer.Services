Plus HVAC Plan

1 AC + 1 Furnace tune-up per year + 10% credits.

What’s Included

  • 1 AC tune-up + 1 Furnace tune-up each year (filter, coils, blower, drains, burners, refrigerant, electrical, safety checks, and a standard filter).
  • Priority scheduling for service requests.
  • 10% service credits toward future repairs/installs (not cash redeemable).

Pricing & Fees

  • $30/month, or annual billing at ~5% discount.
  • $100 onboarding fee (waived with a recent Cramer Services tune-up within 3 months).
  • Discounts are provided for individuals who choose to service multiple units at the same residence.

Is This Plan Right for You?

Perfect for homeowners who want both their furnace and AC serviced at the start of each season—ready for the hottest summer days and coldest winter nights with fewer surprises.

  • You value consistent comfort year-round.
  • Your system is older and you want to prevent unexpected breakdowns.
  • You rely heavily on heating and cooling throughout the year.

Filter guidance

  • We replace a standard filter at each seasonal visit (spring & fall).
  • Between visits, plan to change filters every 1–2 months in higher-demand homes; otherwise every 2–3 months.

Exclusions, Billing & Cancellation

  • Repairs and parts (beyond a standard filter) are not included; quoted separately.
  • Auto-renewing subscription; payments processed automatically.
  • Cancel anytime; no refunds; benefits continue through the current billing period.
