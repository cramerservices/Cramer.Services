Quarterly (4x) system tune-ups + 10% credits.

What’s Included

  • Four full HVAC tune-ups per subscription term; comprehensive checklist (filters, coils, blower, drains, burners, refrigerant, electrical, safety checks).
  • Priority scheduling for service requests.
  • 10% service credits toward future repairs/installs (not cash redeemable).

Pricing & Fees

  • $60/month, or annual billing at ~5% discount.

  • Discounts are provided for individuals who choose to service multiple units at the same residence.

Is This Plan Right for You?

The right choice for small businesses that want their HVAC maintained regularly and a reliable partner to call when issues arise. Consistent service reduces downtime, protects your investment, and keeps customers and staff comfortable.

  • Your property has high foot traffic, long operating hours, or critical uptime needs.
  • The environment is dust-prone (kitchens, salons, shops, warehouses) or you operate multiple packaged/rooftop units.
  • You want proactive documentation for efficiency, safety, and warranty support.

Filter guidance

  • We replace standard filters at each quarterly visit (type/quantity per unit spec).
  • Between visits, plan internal filter changes every 30–60 days; in greasy/dusty spaces, monthly is recommended.

Exclusions, Billing & Cancellation

  • Repairs and parts are not included; quoted separately.
  • Auto-renewing subscription; payments processed automatically.
  • Cancel anytime; no refunds; benefits continue through the current billing period.
