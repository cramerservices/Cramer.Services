Essential HVAC Plan

Annual tune-up + 5% credits toward future work.

What’s Included

  • 1 full HVAC system tune-up per term (inspection, safety checks, coil/blower/drain cleaning, burners, refrigerant, electrical, and a standard filter).
  • Priority scheduling for service requests.
  • 5% service credits applied toward future repairs/installs (not cash redeemable).

Pricing & Fees

  • $20/month, or annual billing at ~5% discount.
  • $125 onboarding fee (waived if you had an HVAC tune-up with us in the past 3 months).
  • Discounts are provided for individuals who choose to service multiple units at the same residence.

Exclusions, Billing & Cancellation

  • Repairs and parts (beyond a standard filter) are not included; quoted separately.
  • Auto-renewing subscription; payments processed automatically.
  • Cancel anytime; no refunds; benefits continue through the current billing period.
