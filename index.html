Who Cramer Services Helps

The Homeowner Facing an Urgent Repair

Your A/C stops cooling, your furnace will not fire, or your system starts making strange noises—and waiting days for help is stressful. You need a reliable HVAC technician who communicates clearly, checks the system, and explains the next steps.

The Small Business Owner with Preventive Maintenance Issues or in Need of HVAC Repairs

Your heating or cooling system is not keeping up, and comfort problems can affect employees and customers. You need a dependable HVAC service provider who can diagnose the issue and help get your space comfortable again.

The Homeowner Preparing for Inspection or Sale

You’re preparing to sell or complete an inspection, and HVAC issues can slow the process down. We can check heating and cooling concerns, recommend repairs, and help make sure your system is ready for the next step.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of services do you offer?▾

We handle A/C repairs, furnace repairs, HVAC diagnostics, tune-ups, system replacements, mini-splits, thermostats, and comfort issues. If it’s not listed, ask—we’ll advise and quote options.

Do you offer warranties or guarantees on your work?▾

Warranty coverage depends on the scope of work, equipment, parts, and manufacturer coverage. We’ll explain warranty details clearly before the job is completed.

Can you handle multiple types of projects at once?▾

Yes. We can handle multiple HVAC-related concerns in one visit, such as A/C performance, furnace issues, thermostat problems, and maintenance items.

How do I request a quote?▾

Click the Contact Us button or call 314-267-8594. Tell us what your system is doing, your address, and your availability—we’ll reply with scheduling options.

Do you offer emergency services?▾

After-hours support may be available depending on technician availability. Click the Contact Us button or call 314-267-8594 to confirm the soonest available appointment.

How soon can you start on my project?▾

Scheduling depends on scope and parts availability. We’ll offer the soonest slots and keep you updated.

How do your subscriptions work?▾

Preventive HVAC maintenance plans are billed yearly and include tune-ups, priority scheduling, and plan discounts or credits depending on the selected plan.

Are your technicians licensed and insured?▾

For licensing, insurance, or project-specific requirements, please contact Cramer Services directly so we can confirm the correct details for your job.

Do you work with both residential and commercial clients?▾

Yes, we provide HVAC service for homeowners, landlords, property managers, and small businesses.
