Frequently Asked Questions

What types of services do you offer?▾

We handle repairs, installations, renovations, maintenance, urgent service requests, and custom requests. If it’s not listed, ask—we’ll advise and quote options.

Do you offer warranties or guarantees on your work?▾

Yes. Labor for installations/renovation is covered for 5 years, while material warranties depend on the products or materials purchased. For repairs, warranty coverage depends on the scope of the work completed. .

Can you handle multiple types of projects at once?▾

Yes. Our all-in-one team coordinates trades so you can bundle tasks in a single plan and schedule.

How do I request a quote?▾

Click the Contact Us button or call 314-267-8594. Share details, materials you prefer, and target dates—we’ll reply with options and pricing.

Do you offer emergency services?▾

After-hours support may be available depending on technician availability. Click the Contact Us button or call 314-267-8594 to confirm the soonest available appointment.

How soon can you start on my project?▾

Scheduling depends on scope and parts availability. We’ll offer the soonest slots and keep you updated.

How do your subscriptions work?▾

Maintenance plans are billed yearly and include two tune-ups per year, priority scheduling, and plan discounts or credits depending on the selected plan.

Are your technicians licensed and insured?▾

For licensing, insurance, or project-specific requirements, please contact Cramer Services directly so we can confirm the correct details for your job.

Do you work with both residential and commercial clients?▾

Yes, we support homeowners, landlords, property managers, and small businesses.
